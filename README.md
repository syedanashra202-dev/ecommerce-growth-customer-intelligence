# E-Commerce Growth & Customer Intelligence Platform

An end-to-end e-commerce analytics project focused on **business performance, customer intelligence, product profitability, and sales-channel analysis**.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes **5,000 e-commerce transactions** to understand:

* Revenue and profit performance
* Product profitability
* Customer behavior and segmentation
* Sales-channel performance
* Regional performance
* Monthly and yearly business trends
* High-value and at-risk customers

The project follows a complete analytics workflow:

**Excel → SQL → Python → Dashboard → Business Insights**

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **MS Excel** — Data validation, PivotTables, business analysis
* **SQL** — KPI analysis, trends, customer and product analysis
* **Python** — Data analysis, visualization and RFM segmentation
* **Power BI / Dashboarding** — Business intelligence and visualization
* **GitHub** — Project documentation and version control

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Electronics Drives Revenue

Electronics generated approximately **₹4.09 crore in revenue**, making it the largest revenue category.

However, its profit margin was approximately **24.65%**, lower than every other major category.

**Business implication:** High revenue does not automatically mean high profitability.

### 2. Revenue Is Highly Concentrated

The top three products were:

* Laptop
* Tablet
* Smartphone

Together they generated approximately **₹3.70 crore**, representing about **61.5% of total revenue**.

**Business implication:** These products are major revenue drivers, but their performance should be monitored because a large portion of revenue depends on a small product group.

### 3. Customer Retention Opportunity

RFM analysis identified:

* **165 At Risk Customers**
* **184 Loyal Customers**
* **10 Champions**
* **769 Lost Customers**

The 165 At Risk customers generated approximately **₹1.12 crore in historical revenue**.

A deeper analysis identified **31 high-value At Risk customers** associated with approximately **₹58.6 lakh in historical revenue**.

**Business implication:** High-value At Risk customers should be prioritized for targeted retention and re-engagement strategies.

### 4. Channel Performance

The Online channel generated the highest revenue at approximately **₹2.16 crore**.

The Store channel had the smallest order volume but a slightly higher profit margin than the other channels.

**Business implication:** Online is the primary scale driver, while channel profitability should be monitored alongside revenue growth.

### 5. Year-over-Year Performance

Revenue decreased by approximately **5.39% from 2023 to 2024**, while profit decreased by approximately **3.92%**.

**Business implication:** The business should investigate the drivers behind the decline and identify opportunities to improve customer retention, product performance, and revenue growth.

## 👥 Customer Intelligence

RFM segmentation was used to classify customers based on:

* **Recency** — How recently the customer purchased
* **Frequency** — How often the customer purchased
* **Monetary Value** — How much the customer spent

Customer segments include:

* Champions
* Loyal Customers
* Potential Loyalists
* At Risk Customers
* Needs Attention
* Lost Customers

## 📊 Dashboard

## 🧠 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. Prioritize retention campaigns for high-value At Risk customers.
2. Monitor profitability of high-revenue Electronics products.
3. Reduce dependence on a small number of top products by developing profitable secondary products.
4. Investigate the 2024 revenue decline using product, customer, channel and regional trends.
5. Use RFM segmentation to personalize customer marketing strategies.
6. Monitor revenue and profit together rather than focusing only on sales volume.
